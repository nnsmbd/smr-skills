# Social Core

Social content expresses the shared voice under platform constraints. Platform norms affect length, sequencing, formatting, and CTA; they do not prove Samir's style.

## Shared behavior

- Lead with one concrete reason to stop.
- Keep one primary idea per post or clearly connected sequence.
- Preserve a real personal detail when the source contains one; never invent a scene.
- Make the post useful without requiring the reader to click through.
- Link to a canonical article only when a real link and traffic goal exist.
- Do not attach `подпишись`, `что думаете?`, or a manufactured debate prompt mechanically.

## Cross-posting

Choose `verbatim`, `adapted`, or `teaser`. All adaptations must derive from the same content packet. Do not compress the Telegram version into Instagram and then compress Instagram into X.

## Evidence boundary

Only user-written or explicitly approved material from a platform may establish that platform's voice override. Until then, use the shared voice plus conservative platform conventions and mark the result provisional.
