# Site Blog

Evidence status: provisional. Current evidence includes Samir's explicit structural feedback and an edited draft, but no approved final site-article corpus yet. Use long-form editorial judgment without presenting generic conventions as Samir's proven style.

## Job

Develop one central promise with enough context, evidence, explanation, and personal position to reward a full read. A site article may become the canonical source for later social adaptations.

## Structure

- Make the title's promise govern the section order.
- Open with a concrete scene, result, problem, or tension rather than generic framing.
- Give every section a distinct job; remove repository facts that do not advance the story.
- Use technical detail to explain behavior, decisions, or consequences.
- Prefer a few developed examples over an exhaustive audit trail.
- End with a changed understanding, next experiment, or proportionate action.

## Task Tracker evidence

For a product case study, prefer:

`hook → personal need → what the product became → human-language architecture → major capabilities → where AI helped → limitations or next improvements → lessons`.

Do not substitute `commit history → bug list → technical debt inventory` merely because those facts are available.

## Voice

Allow more explanation than in social content, but preserve Samir's reactions, motivations, and decisions. Do not insert jokes solely to interrupt technical prose. If a section reads like documentation, add missing author reasoning rather than a decorative punchline.

## Publishing and provenance

Mark a site article `canonical: true` only when it is the named source for adaptations. A draft with embedded comments is raw evidence, not a normalized anchor or publishable sample.
