# Instagram

Evidence status: provisional; no approved Instagram corpus is stored yet.

## Default adaptation

First determine the asset: caption, carousel script, Reel caption, or Story sequence. If missing and materially important, ask.

- For a caption, preserve one scene or conflict and one takeaway.
- For a carousel, give each card one job and ensure the first card contains a real promise.
- Make captions self-contained; do not rely on unexplained visuals.
- Keep the personal voice, but do not manufacture lifestyle intimacy or inspirational conclusions.
- Avoid hashtag blocks unless requested or supported by a distribution strategy.

Do not record these platform defaults as Samir-specific voice cues until user-edited examples exist.
