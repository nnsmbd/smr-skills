# Sales Reference Patterns

These pages were supplied as references. Analyze mechanisms and tradeoffs; do not copy wording, structure, claims, or design blindly. Treat authorship and preference as unconfirmed until Samir labels them.

## DavaBlogg — short lead-generation service page

Source: https://davablogg.pulse.is/

Useful mechanisms:

- quantified outcome appears immediately;
- service is explained through audience research, creative production, media buying, and funnel improvement;
- experience, geography, managed budget, reporting, and guarantee reduce perceived risk;
- consultation CTA repeats after proof.

Watchouts:

- every lead volume, payback, budget, experience, and guarantee claim needs current evidence and exact terms;
- phrases such as `hot leads ready to buy now` require operational definitions;
- generic reliability language is weaker than case evidence.

## Serditov — long-form direct response

Source: https://serditov.ru/bsvr

Useful mechanisms:

- strong contrarian opening;
- repeated myth-versus-mechanism contrast;
- personal results and story create authority;
- objections are handled throughout instead of saved only for FAQ;
- offer appears after substantial belief-building.

Watchouts:

- aggression, profanity, sexual humor, and pressure are not Samir's default voice;
- passive-income and outsized-result claims need exceptional proof and careful framing;
- repeated rhetorical questions and all-caps can exhaust readers;
- copy the sequencing logic only when it fits the audience and offer.

## Petrochenkov — expert system offer

Source: https://petrochenkov.online/

Useful mechanisms:

- frames the product as an operating system rather than isolated tactics;
- names concrete outputs, modules, implementation steps, and team support;
- uses authority, cases, numbers, templates, and bonuses to reduce uncertainty;
- contrasts the current broken state with a controlled future state.

Watchouts:

- long inventories can obscure the primary transformation;
- repeated benefits and credentials need hierarchy;
- every case metric, budget, ranking, and outcome must be traceable.

## Resulting — reputation and legacy

Source: https://grebenukresulting.ru/

Useful mechanisms:

- leads with history, meaning, and people;
- combines emotion with a large body of concrete proof;
- demonstrates breadth through niches, geography, clients, and aggregate outcomes;
- thanks the people involved instead of turning the entire page into a pitch.

Watchouts:

- this is not a standard conversion page and should not be forced into a direct-response template;
- proof volume needs navigation and prioritization;
- aggregate numbers still require documented methodology.

## Selection rule

- Choose DavaBlogg-like compactness for a clear service with ready proof and one CTA.
- Choose Petrochenkov-like system explanation for complex B2B transformation or education.
- Choose long-form direct response only when traffic temperature, offer economics, and evidence justify it.
- Choose Resulting-like narrative for trust, history, closure, reputation, or brand capital.
- Combine mechanisms only when each section has a distinct job. Do not produce a collage of every reference.
